Camera setup matters more than the model

Put the phone or laptop on a tripod or a chair, side-on to the player, roughly waist height, 6–15 feet away, with the whole body and the floor in frame. Bright even light, no backlight. A handheld camera wrecks the step counter, because the referee cannot tell your movement from the player's.

Read this before you trust it

This is a computer-vision toy, not an officiating system. It infers rules from a single 2D camera, so it misses violations and invents ones that did not happen. Fouls are not detectable this way at all. Use it for drills and feedback, never to settle an argument in a real game.
